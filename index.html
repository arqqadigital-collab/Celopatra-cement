Who We Are

Egypt’s Premier White Cement Producer

Established in 2018, Cleopatra Cement operates one of the region’s most established white cement plants, backed by more than three decades of manufacturing expertise and excellence. Today, the company proudly serves clients across the Middle East, Africa, Europe, and North America with high-performance white cement solutions.

With a reputation built on superior whiteness, consistent quality, and innovation, Cleopatra Cement continues to set new benchmarks in the white cement industry.

Sustainability Building Responsibly

Sustainability is a foundational principle at Cleopatra Cement. We continuously invest in green technologies and environmentally responsible practices to reduce emissions, conserve energy, and optimize resources.

Renewable Energy Solutions

Transitioning to zero-carbon energy with solar networks and wind integration to fuel smart plant setups.

Alternative Fuels

Replacing fossil materials with co-processed secure components for optimal thermal clinker steps.

ESG Reporting

Pioneering standard disclosures in governance, emissions limits, and green performance targets.

Energy Efficiency Programs

Incorporating innovative Waste Heat Recovery (WHR) loops to preserve vital grid energy.

Green Hydrogen Exploration

Studying clean hydrogen fuel applications to achieve zero emissions thermal systems.

Digital & AI Integration

Using machine learning and real-time sensor analytics to maximize kiln heat output.
